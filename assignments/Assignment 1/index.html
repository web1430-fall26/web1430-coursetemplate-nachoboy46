BEST MAGIC CARDS

  • Captain America Living Legend

CAPTAIN AMERICA LIVING LEGEND

Captain America is a fantastic card. He's currently my favorite commander. Tapping is such a core part of magic the gathering, being able to do that twice a turn per creature is just so powerful.

ORNITHOPTER OF PARADISE

Ornithopter of paradise pairs really well with captain america. It's already a great manadork for every non-green color, but where cap let's it tap twice it's just incredible.

COMMAND TOWER

What can I say. Everybody loves a good command tower. Plus, the art is freaking dope.
